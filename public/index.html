Welcome to AgroMagic

More than a farm.
A place to belong.

We believe good things grow when people come together. On ten acres in Stockbridge, we're dreaming up a self-sustaining farm and campground that also makes room for art, learning, and connection.

It's an idea in the making, and there's room for you in it. Whether you love getting your hands in the soil, sharing a skill, making art, or simply helping a good idea grow, we hope you'll be part of the journey.
