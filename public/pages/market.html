A little market with a big mission.

We're planning a place to share and sell items that help support the growth of AgroMagic.

Good things, doing good.

We believe the things we make and share can help a community grow. The AgroMagic market is part of our vision for raising funds and building a more self-sustaining project, while celebrating the creativity and work of the people around us.

The market is not open yet, and products, prices, shipping, and pickup details have not been announced. As the project develops, we'll share what's available and explain how purchases support our work.

Have something to contribute?

Are you a maker, artist, or grower interested in the possibilities? We'd love to connect with people who want to help shape the market and the creative community around it.
