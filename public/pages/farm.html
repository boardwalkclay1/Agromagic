Building The Pharm

Growing Food. Growing Hope.

The Pharm is our immediate mission. This is where we're beginning to grow food, host gatherings, teach workshops, build community, and create the foundation for everything AgroMagic will become.

What We're Building

A Living Community Sanctuary.

The Pharm is more than a farm. It is becoming a place where people can learn, create, heal, connect, and build a stronger future together.

🌱

Growing Food

Our first priority is beginning production on the land. We are investing in seeds, soil, beds, tools, irrigation, and sustainable growing systems.

🔥

Bonfires & Events

The Pharm will host bonfires, music, gatherings, celebrations, and community events that bring people together.

🛠️

Workshops

Hands-on learning opportunities covering farming, homesteading, construction, bushcraft, and practical life skills.

🤝

Community

Every day we are building something bigger than ourselves, and there is room for volunteers and contributors to help shape the future.

Our Long-Term Vision

Modern Bushcraft Tiny Homes.

One of our future goals is to build modern bushcraft-inspired tiny homes that blend thoughtful design, sustainability, craftsmanship, and comfort.

These tiny homes will help create a unique destination where people can retreat, reconnect with nature, learn new skills, and find peace away from the pressures of everyday life.

Vision A sanctuary for our community and anyone who needs a safe place, a helping hand, or a fresh start.
