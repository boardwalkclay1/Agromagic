The idea behind the magic

A place to grow, create, and belong.

AgroMagic is a vision for bringing people and possibilities together on ten acres in Stockbridge, Georgia.

It starts with the land.

We imagine a place where growing food, caring for the earth, sharing knowledge, and making art are all part of the same story. A place where people can contribute what they know, learn something new, and feel connected to one another.

AgroMagic brings together a self-sustaining farm, a future campground, workshops, and a home for creators and performances. Each part supports the bigger idea: a community that grows more resourceful, creative, and connected together.

What self-sustaining means to us

Our goal is to build thoughtfully over time: growing food, making careful use of resources, sharing skills, and developing ways for the project to support itself. It is an aspiration, not a claim that the farm is already self-sufficient. We expect to learn and adapt as the project develops.

Built with community, not just for it

Many hands and perspectives can shape what AgroMagic becomes. Volunteers, growers, artists, teachers, neighbors, supporters, and curious beginners all have something valuable to bring. We're at the beginning, and we welcome people who want to help bring the vision to life.
