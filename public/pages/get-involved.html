There's a place for you here.

Big dreams take a community. Bring your skills, your curiosity, and your willingness to grow alongside us.

Let's build this together.

AgroMagic is in its early stages, and we're looking for people who want to help bring the vision to life. You don't have to be an expert. Whether you have a little time, a particular skill, or an idea to share, there may be a way for you to contribute as the project develops.

Potential needs include preparing and caring for the land, growing food, building and maintaining spaces, organizing gatherings, teaching skills, and sharing creative work. Opportunities will depend on the project's stage and needs.

Interested in volunteering?

Tell us a little about yourself and what you'd like to do. When signups are open, this form will prepare an email for you to review and send. We'll share information about next steps and opportunities as they become available.

Signups require JavaScript and a configured contact address.

Every contribution matters

Not sure where you fit?

That's okay. Enthusiasm, a willingness to learn, and a desire to help are a great place to start. Tell us what interests you and we can explore the possibilities together.

For the makers and dreamers

Make space for creativity.

We want AgroMagic to be a place where artists, makers, musicians, performers, and other creators can connect, share their work, and inspire one another. We imagine creative gatherings and performances alongside the farm and future campground.

These spaces and programs are still being imagined, so there are no scheduled performances or bookings yet. If you'd like to help shape them, use the interest form above when signups open and tell us about your work.
